Research

Geometry, topology and applications in data & life sciences

  • topological data analysis and computational topology
  • geometric and topological methods in omics data analysis
  • bounded cohomology and simplicial volume
  • Higgs bundle moduli spaces, harmonic maps and representation varieties
  • geometry of special functions (polylogarithms, hypergeometric functions)

I am a member of the Helmholtz Information & Data Science School for Health HIDSS4Health and the DFG Priority Program Geometry at Infinity.
